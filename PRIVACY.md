# Flightstead Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

Flightstead is an iPhone app for publishing to a Flightstead server that you choose and control. The app connects directly to that server. Flightstead does not provide a hosted account service and the app developer does not receive the content or account information you send to your server.

## Data collection

The Flightstead app does not collect data for the app developer. It does not include advertising, tracking, analytics, or third-party telemetry SDKs.

Information you enter or select in the app—including your server address, email address, posts, photographs, profile information, and authentication data—is stored on your device as needed to provide the app and is transmitted directly to the Flightstead server you configured. The operator of that server controls its storage, retention, and deletion practices.

## Device features

Flightstead uses the camera only when you choose to scan a server-pairing QR code. It accesses photographs only when you select them for publishing. Drafts and pending uploads may be stored locally on your device until they are published or removed.

## Optional tips

Optional tips are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Flightstead verifies purchases on the device and does not receive or store your payment-card information. Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

## Self-hosted servers

Your chosen server may process information differently depending on how it is configured and operated. Consult the server operator's privacy information if you do not operate the server yourself.
